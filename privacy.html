Privacy policy

Angel Army · Last updated 26 October 2026

Angel Army is a private personal assistant. It is used only by its owner, Gilbert, with his own Google accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reads the owner's email to find bills, receipts, reminders and messages that need attention. It sends email only when the owner explicitly asks, and only from the owner's own account.
  • Google Calendar: reads and creates events and reminders in the owner's calendar.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: creates and updates the owner's own documents and spreadsheets (for example a personal finance sheet and saved invoices).
  • Contacts: reads names and birthdays to remind the owner of them.

How the data is used

Only to provide these features to the owner: reminders, finance tracking, search and summaries. Data stays in the owner's Google account and on a private server controlled by the owner.

What it never does

  • Sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • Use Google user data for advertising.
  • Use Google user data to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Let any person other than the owner read the data.

Google API Services

Angel Army's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account: Security, then Third-party connections (myaccount.google.com/connections).
